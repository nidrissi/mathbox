# Final reconciliation

Read the [shared protocol](review-protocol.md), full manuscript, coverage inventory, raw findings,
and the specialist evidence bearing on important concerns. These are evidence,
not verdicts. Final synthesis must inspect the manuscript itself.

1. Search for each important finding's exact quote and read its surrounding
   definitions, hypotheses, argument and dependencies. If it cannot be
   located, resolve the revision/quote mismatch rather than endorsing it.
2. Independently reconstruct the alleged failed implication or admissible
   case. Read serious `proof-audit`, source and computation checks; an audit
   label alone is not confirmation. Preserve conditional external leaves.
3. Resolve cross-section suspicions against the full source. If notation or
   a convention was supplied earlier, dismiss the absent-definition finding;
   do not hedge it into an author question. Check later proofs and appendices
   before claiming a result or promise is missing.
4. Remove routine-algebra overflagging, ordinary compression, taste preferences
   and unsupported leads. Recheck high-confidence findings too. Confidence
   under $0.5$ warrants investigation only when consequential, not automatic
   inclusion. Silence after proper checking is valid.
5. Merge duplicate descriptions of the same defect, preserving additional
   locations and dependency consequences. Several lanes pointing to one
   missing hypothesis produce one critique. Distinct obligations in one proof
   may remain distinct. Judge severity by impact, not finding count or votes.
6. Recalibrate severity and confidence after checking. A gap, a false statement,
   a missing local explanation and an unverified external input are different
   conclusions. Name your own observations and check them by the same rules.
7. Record retained/dismissed/merged/conditional/stale dispositions, checked
   locations and reasons using [output-contract.md](output-contract.md).
   Preserve raw returns. Do not change them to match the final report.
8. Synthesize the report. Confirmed mathematical concerns from any lane of critical/major
   severity lead the mathematical concerns; local mathematical defects go in
   other mathematical concerns. Exposition always stays in exposition;
   claim calibration in claim/scope assessment, cross-linked to mathematical
   impact when needed. Unsettled substantive obligations belong in questions
   with their decisive check, not insinuations of established error.

State actual coverage and failures in the Summary. Partial extraction,
unreviewed units or unavailable evidence preclude a complete verification
claim. Describe checks that actually occurred, and what was not settled.
No report certifies universal correctness or novelty from absent findings.
Do not claim an external result is known without verified primary evidence.

The overall assessment weighs the established contribution, central concerns
and coverage. Do not infer a journal's standards or publication recommendation
unless the user supplied that context. A requested recommendation may be
accept, minor revisions, major revisions or reject, with concrete reasons;
otherwise give a calibrated manuscript assessment. Do not manufacture
questions or revisions just to fill headings.
