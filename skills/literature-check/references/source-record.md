For priority inquiries, use the search scope and coverage gaps to bound the
earliest qualifying proof located. In the bibliographic record, distinguish
the date of the inspected proof version from its publication date. In the
caveats, give the evidence for any claim to first publication or historical
priority, or leave that question open.

The Model ID names the author of this source assessment, not the paper's author
or the cache software; see [attribution.md](attribution.md). Each source record
is an immutable entry even inside a shared literature file: a new assessment or
correction is a new record with its own author's ID, and the registry table
carries none.
