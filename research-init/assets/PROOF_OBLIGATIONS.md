## Status vocabulary

Use: **proved**, **externally proved**, **computationally verified**,
**heuristic**, **conjectural**, **conditional**, or **open/failed**.

When a claim changes, preserve the stable ID where possible and append the
correction to `RESEARCH_LOG.md`. Do not turn this table into a historical diary.
